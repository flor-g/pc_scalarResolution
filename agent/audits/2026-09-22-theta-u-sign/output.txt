1.  WHY THE SIGN IS NOT A PROPERTY OF THE ENTRY.  The ensemble is {chi, ker chi} and
    chi + ker chi = 1 exactly, so sum_y phi_L,y = Lambda * 1 and
        sum_y c_y = B^T W (2 ell_0 - Lambda * 1) = 2 B^T W ell_0,
    because B^T W 1 = 0 (the basis is orthogonal to the constant). Eq. (B2) gives theta_u*
    the sign of <mu_u, sum c>, so THAT SIGN IS FIXED BY THE PRIOR ALONE -- independent of
    Lambda and of which entry was uttered.
====================================================================================================

    max |sum_y c_y  -  2 B^T W ell_0| over 4 Lambda x 2 classes x 6 items: 5.04e-13
