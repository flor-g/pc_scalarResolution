    (1 -> 3, and 3 -> 7 for the delta-like row). At 1e-2 the rows that are not
    slow-started go up 11 to 17 fold. At 1e-1 the comparison is contaminated by
    slow starts (flat at Lambda = 8, skewed low at Lambda = 512) and is not monotone.

    At Lambda = 512 the flow halts at 3.5% to 5% of theta* under EVERY prior and
    across a tenfold change of tolerance. At Lambda = 8 the same tolerances give
    anywhere from 0% to 30%. So at large Lambda the tolerance barely moves where the
    flow lands, which is the sharper form of the Lambda dependence.
